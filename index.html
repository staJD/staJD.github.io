StajPilot

Flash your controller straight from the browser — no software to install.

 

    This browser can't flash over USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (phones and Safari aren't supported).
    Firmware for this board isn't published yet — check back soon.

    Questions

    My computer doesn't see the device

    Try a different USB cable — some cables are charge-only and can't carry data. Also try a different USB port directly on your computer rather than through a hub.

    The flash failed partway through

    Unplug the board, plug it back in, and try again from the start. Don't disconnect the cable while a flash is in progress.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No. Flashing happens directly between your browser and the board over USB — the firmware file never leaves your computer except to download it from this page.
